    Win the best-of-three series and take the dawn.

    Sorin's mood:

    Mouse or keys 1–5 to pick · Enter to clash · F to focus · Esc to pause · M sound · Fully offline, no network.

    📖 How to play

    ⚔ The clashEach round, you and Sorin each play one card face down, then reveal together. Higher Power deals the difference + 2 as damage — a won clash always bites. First duelist to 0 life loses the duel; win 2 duels to take the match.
    🔆 The wheelFour lights counter in a circle: Ember ▸ Grove ▸ Tide ▸ Bolt ▸ Ember. Playing the light that beats Sorin's gives +3 Power — the cheapest damage in the game.
    ☀ Sun energyCards cost Sun (top-right of the card). You gain +1 Sun each round, banking up to 5. No affordable card? Focus: discard a card for +1 Sun. Big cards are worth banking for.
    🔮 Read SorinThe ribbon under Sorin shows the element he is leaning toward — his likeliest pick, honestly told. He studies your last three cards and counters them; vary your lights or he will farm you. His taunts tell you how he feels about the table.
    ✨ SkillsEvery one of the 24 cards carries its own unique skill — read the gold line on the card. Spark Bite singes on reveal, Thorns bites back on a loss, Chain Lightning grows with your wins, Rebirth refuses to die. Sorin's skills fire too: watch what he plays.
    🏆 Score & ranksDamage, won clashes, duels and life left all score. Win 2 clashes in a row and Radiance feeds you +1 Sun. Your best score, crowns and rank — Spark Novice up to Aurora Sovereign — are kept between visits.

    🃏 The twenty-four lights

    Every card in both decks — one copy each, and no two share a skill. Costs are Sun; the big number is Power; the gold line is the skill.
